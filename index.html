How I work

Evidence before investment.

The same four steps sit behind every product I ship. They are how 10 pilots became 8 scaled products — and why 2 were stopped before they cost more.

Discover

Start with an unmet workflow, not a feature request. Sit with the finance team that feels the pain.

Decide

Build, buy or partner — settled on capability, economics, risk and customer fit, in writing.

Pilot

Ship the smallest real version to 3–5 accounts and measure adoption and payment success.

Scale

Make the go/no-go call on evidence, then roll out and own the revenue line.
